18+Years in retail technology

I have spent more than two decades in technology, with deep experience in retail technology and a long chapter at Target spanning enterprise strategy, innovation, R&D and transformation.

That experience gave me a front-row seat to how large organisations make technology choices, build teams, scale engineering and navigate change. It also taught me that the most interesting problems are rarely solved by technology alone.

Today I increasingly choose to work alongside founders and ambitious teams — bringing an enterprise lens, pattern recognition, strategic challenge and a willingness to get close to the problem.

CURA is the home for that next chapter.

I believe in transparency, empowerment and leading by example. I value situations over labels, outcomes over activity and integrity over convenience.

But above all, I believe passion wins over many things. That is why shared passion is one of the strongest filters I use before choosing where to spend my time.

CURA is deliberately not a catalogue of services. It is a record of the companies, ideas and people that earn my conviction.
