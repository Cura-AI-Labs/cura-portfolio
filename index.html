I don't partner because a company needs an advisor. I partner when I believe I can genuinely change the trajectory — and care enough to wake up and put the time in.

01

Solve a Need

Needs over wants. Clear boundaries, specific stakeholders, meaningful TAM — and a deep solve over a “solve everything” proposition.

02

Believe in the Builders

Passion, research, domain learning and a team that brings credibility to the solution. With room, occasionally, for a bold bet.

03

Follow the Conviction

Scale and impact matter. So does instinct. Sometimes a problem simply speaks to me.

04

Shared Passion Matters

I need to believe in the core. Network, experience and capital are secondary to wanting to put my time behind it.

05

Partnership is Mutual

Trust, empowerment and open perspectives are fundamental. If the team doesn't align, nothing else matters.

06

Know When to Walk Away

I don't resonate with founders who undervalue strategic contribution, chase growth at any cost or confuse burning capital with building a business.
