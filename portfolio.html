01 / The Portfolio

Future problems
worth shaping.

These are not investments on a spreadsheet. They are problems, people and possibilities where I have chosen to spend time, conviction and intellectual capital.

01
AI · AGENT IDENTITY · SECURITY

AgentID

The agentic enterprise needs trust before it needs scale.

AgentID passed my strategic filter: an emerging need, a focused problem, a strong founding team and a product ahead of where many enterprises are today. The shift from siloed to integrated agents creates a new need for agent-native management while still integrating with the broader enterprise security ecosystem.

My role is to strategically position AgentID for partnerships and challenge the team on adjacent opportunities. I see a path toward agent communication moderation, failure visibility and prevention of rogue behaviour — ultimately giving customers confidence that their data and transactions are safe.

MY BET If AgentID gets its positioning and technology right, it can accelerate trust between agents, enterprises and customers.
02
SUPPLY CHAIN · DECISION INTELLIGENCE

LogiModel

Making supply-chain intelligence matter where it matters most.

Supply chains are the arteries of everyday life and livelihoods. What drew me in was the gravity of the problem, the credibility of the founders and the depth of IP behind a platform built on years of research across complex supply-chain environments.

LogiModel makes best-in-class technology available to the people who actually make the difference, solving problems in their backyard rather than pushing intelligence away into technology teams. This is a high-trust partnership where I am helping lead the India strategy — on the ground and in the boardroom.

Its potential is tangible: more accurate forecasting, faster delivery and predictable inventory can create enterprise value and ultimately impact customers' lives. The IP must keep evolving with market realities and geopolitical shifts.

MY BET If LogiModel builds a defensible MOAT, scales and keeps driving value for everyone, it can become a differentiated intelligence layer for medium and large enterprises.
03
SPACETECH · TALENT · INDIA

Zeta Space Launchpad

Making SpaceTech a technology discipline, not a distant dream.

Launchpad sits at the intersection of talent development, building for the future and the extraordinary potential of SpaceTech from an Indian context. A trusted referral, a passionate team and strong domain experts from ISRO made the opportunity feel like things falling into place.

I see a gap in the narrative: we celebrate India's space achievements, but private talent still isn't always seen as a central part of the story. The time is prime for foundational investment in curiosity, confidence, access to experts and meaningful challenges.

My aspiration is to take the movement to the grassroots while helping build a scalable, outcome-led GTM model. And there is a personal 20-year horizon: I want a future where a child can see SpaceTech as naturally as software engineering or AI.

MY BET If Launchpad gets the narrative and outcomes right, it can make SpaceTech just another technology topic — like AI is today.
04
AI · RESEARCH · AGENTIC ORCHESTRATION

CMUL8

From research DNA to the foundations of trustworthy agentic societies.

What drew me to CMUL8 is the research pedigree of its founding team. They care about what they build, take pride in their solutions and have the curiosity to seek mentorship. I see an opportunity to help exceptional AI researchers channel their energy into differentiated enterprise products.

The team is trying to realise the value of research papers and frameworks faster, cheaper and more reliably. Their differentiator is simple: Research DNA, but End-User Products and Problems.

I want to help them make bold bets around multi-agent orchestration — looking beyond today's reality and shaping an enterprise foundation for what agents can become. The watch-out is convergence: I constantly challenge the team to show up as entrepreneurs, not academicians.

MY BET If CMUL8 gets agentic orchestration right, it can help build trustworthy agentic societies that can co-exist.
