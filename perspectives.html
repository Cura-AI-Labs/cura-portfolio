Make sense of
what is moving.

Short observations from the intersection of technology, business, leadership and the builders trying to change both.

AI · 2026

The enterprise doesn't need more AI. It needs better decisions about where AI belongs.

The interesting question is shifting from “Can AI do this?” to “Should this decision be made by AI, by a human, or by both?”

LEADERSHIP · 2026

Scale is not the ability to do more. It is the ability to stay intentional while doing more.

Systems, culture and operating rhythm become the real differentiators when organisations move beyond the first wave of transformation.

STARTUPS · 2026

The best founders don't sell certainty. They create conviction in the face of uncertainty.

Early-stage building is less about having all the answers and more about making the next important question visible.
